Privacy

FBG START LTD · Updated 6 September 2026

The short version

Settled does not require an account and contains no third-party advertising or app analytics SDK. App selections, usage counts, goals, activity history and notes are processed and stored on your phone. Choosing to contact support sends information outside your phone, as explained below.

Permissions and on-device processing

On Android, usage access and the optional accessibility service identify the foreground app to apply your limits. The accessibility service does not read messages, passwords or screen content. On iPhone, Screen Time authorization lets Settled monitor and shield the apps you choose. Motion access supports walking and running checks. Notifications and alarm permissions support the timers and alarms you configure.

When you send feedback

A problem report or leaving note sends the words you provide, app version and platform to support. Depending on the app version, a report may also include device model, operating-system version and recent alarm diagnostics to help investigate problems. Do not include information you do not want support to receive.

Reports are delivered through Google Firebase and our email provider, Resend. Newer versions retain unsent reports on-device and retry when a connection is available. We use reports to respond and improve Settled and retain them only as long as needed for those purposes. Contact support to request deletion.

Purchases

Apple or Google processes payments and keeps the purchase record. Settled checks entitlement status with the store and retains local access flags. We do not receive your card details. Prices and subscription terms are displayed in the store checkout.

Website measurement

This site counts store-button clicks by day, channel, campaign, store and button placement. It does not set tracking cookies, create visitor profiles or collect names or email addresses for these counts. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals disable this click measurement. Store links carry campaign parameters so the stores can report attribution where supported. Button clicks are not installs or purchases.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages; the counter uses Google Firebase. Hosting providers process technical request information, including IP addresses, in their operational and security logs. Our counter stores only aggregate counts, not IP addresses or user-agent strings. Counts may include repeat clicks or bots and are not unique-user measurements.

Earlier launch-list subscriptions

The launch waitlist is no longer needed to download Settled. If you previously subscribed, the address you submitted through Google Forms remains separate from app usage. Contact support to unsubscribe or request deletion. Removing the form does not itself delete earlier submissions.

Removing local data

Uninstalling removes the app's local working data. Store purchase records remain with Apple or Google, and previously submitted support messages are separate. A trial-start marker may persist through Android device backup or iOS Keychain so reinstalling does not restart introductory access. Device backup and account services are controlled by your device provider.

Your choices and rights

You can change app permissions in your phone's settings. You can ask us to access, correct or delete personal information you have sent to us by emailing support@thesettledapp.com. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
